How to play

Goal

Turn the tiles so every pipe joins up into one network, with no loose ends.

How it works

  • Tap a tile to turn it a quarter turn clockwise. Tiles can't be moved, only turned.
  • Water flows from the peach source tile. Pipes connected to it light up blue.
  • You've solved it when every tile is lit and no pipe end points at a wall or at a pipe that doesn't meet it.
  • Each turn counts as a move. Every network has a solution.

Tips

  • Start at the edges and corners. A pipe end can never point off the board, which often leaves only one way a tile can face.
  • A straight pipe on an edge must run along the edge.
  • The network never makes a loop, so if two pipes would close a circle, one of them must turn.

A little history

Pipe-connecting puzzles became popular on computers and phones in the 1990s and 2000s. This rotating-tile version, sometimes called Net, is a classic of free puzzle collections.
