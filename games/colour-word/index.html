How to play

Goal

A colour name appears in coloured ink. Tap the colour of the ink, not the word. Try to get all ten right.

How it works

  • You will see a word such as Blue, but it may be written in red ink. The right answer is then Red.
  • Tap the button that matches the ink. A game is ten rounds.
  • There is no clock, so take your time. Your score is how many you got right.
  • Choose 3 colours to start, or 4 for a bigger challenge.

Tips

  • Your eyes will want to read the word. That pull is normal, and it is what the game trains you to resist.
  • Try to ignore what the letters say and just look at the colour, as if the word were a blob.
  • Slow, steady answers beat rushed ones.

The history of Colour Word

In 1935 the American psychologist John Ridley Stroop showed that people name the ink colour of a word more slowly when the word spells a different colour. The effect is named after him and is still one of the most studied results in psychology: it shows how reading is so automatic that it gets in the way. Researchers use versions of it to study attention and self-control.
