Make 24

How to play

Goal

Use all four numbers, each exactly once, with adding, subtracting, multiplying and dividing to make 24.

How it works

  • Tap a number, then a sign (+ − × ÷), then a second number. The two are replaced by the answer.
  • Keep combining until one number is left. If it's 24, you've solved it.
  • Undo takes back your last step. “Show me” reveals one way to do it, and a new deal follows.
  • Easy deals can always be solved without dividing. Tricky deals need division, and sometimes fractions along the way.
  • Every deal has at least one solution.

Example

With 1, 2, 3 and 4: 1 + 2 = 3, then 3 + 3 = 6, then 6 × 4 = 24.

Tips

  • Know the friendly pairs that make 24: 3 × 8, 4 × 6, 2 × 12, 1 × 24, and 12 + 12, 20 + 4, 30 − 6.
  • Look for a way to make 8 with three numbers when you hold a 3, or 6 when you hold a 4.
  • A 1 is handy: multiplying or dividing by it changes nothing, so use it to tidy up.
  • Fractions are allowed in the middle. For example 8 ÷ (3 − 8 ÷ 3) = 24.

A little history

Making 24 from four playing cards is a popular game across China, where it is often called 24 points (èrshísì diǎn) and played in schools to sharpen mental arithmetic. Similar card games are played around the world.
