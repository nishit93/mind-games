How to play

Goal

Find the friend who is hiding behind one of the hiding places.

How it works

  • A friend is hiding behind one of the trees, boxes or plants. Tap a hiding place to peek behind it.
  • If nobody is there, it turns grey, so you know you have checked it. Try another one.
  • When you find the friend, you move on to the next round, with more places to look.
  • Find the friend in all six rounds to win.

Tips

  • Check every place once. The greyed-out ones are done.
  • There is no clever trick: the friend could be anywhere, so start where you like.
  • Count your peeks, and see if you can find them in fewer next time.

The history of Hide and Seek

Hide and seek is one of the oldest children's games and is played all over the world, with names like "peekaboo" for the youngest players. This version is a quiet, single-player take on it.
