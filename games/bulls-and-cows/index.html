Guesses 0 Best –
    How to play

    Goal

    Work out the secret number in as few guesses as you can.

    How it works

    • Choose a 3- or 4-digit secret with the buttons below the keypad. No digit appears twice (for example 4071), and it can start with 0.
    • Type a guess with the keypad (or your keyboard) and press Guess. Your guess must also use different digits.
    • Each guess is scored:
      • Bull (filled dot): a right digit in the right place.
      • Cow (ring): a right digit in the wrong place.
    • The dots don't say which digits scored, only how many. Four bulls means you've cracked it.

    Example

    The secret is 4071 and you guess 1074. The 0 and the 7 are in the right places (2 bulls). The 1 and the 4 are in the secret but in the wrong places (2 cows).

    Tips

    • Start with guesses that share no digits, like 0123 then 4567. Their scores tell you which digits are in the secret.
    • A guess scoring nothing at all is useful: it rules out every one of its digits.
    • Change one or two digits at a time to find out which ones scored.

    The history of Bulls & Cows

    Bulls and Cows is a pencil-and-paper game said to be over a century old, often traced to Britain. It inspired the 1970 board game Mastermind, which uses coloured pegs instead of digits.
