How to play

Goal

Find every one of the picture shown at the bottom, hiding among the other things.

How it works

  • The message under the board shows what to look for, such as ⭐. Tap every one you can find.
  • Each one you find gets a green ring, and a counter shows how many are left.
  • Tapping something else just wiggles it. There is no timer.
  • Find them all to move on. Six boards make a game.

Tips

  • Move across the board in rows, like reading a page.
  • Count along as you go: "one, two…" helps you remember.
  • Check the corners at the end, where things like to hide.

The history of Find Them All

Games of finding every hidden object in a crowded picture have been a favourite of children and puzzle books for a long time. They sharpen attention to detail and are good practice for counting.
