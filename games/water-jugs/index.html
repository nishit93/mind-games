How to play

Goal

Measure out an exact amount of water using jugs that have no markings: get the target amount into any one jug.

How it works

  • Pour: tap one jug, then another. Water pours from the first into the second until the first is empty or the second is full.
  • Fill and Empty: on levels with a tap, each jug has Fill and Empty buttons. On levels without a tap, the water you start with is all you have.
  • The number on each jug is how much water it holds now; the number below is how much it can hold.
  • Each pour, fill or empty is one move. Try to match the fewest possible.

Tips

  • A full jug poured into a smaller one leaves the difference behind. With jugs of 5 and 3, filling the 5 and pouring into the 3 leaves exactly 2.
  • Keep a pattern going: fill the big jug, pour into the small one, empty the small one, pour again. This cycles through every amount you can make.
  • You can only make amounts that are multiples of the largest number dividing both jug sizes, so think about which jug sizes work together.

A little history

Pouring puzzles appear in the work of Italian mathematician Niccolò Tartaglia in the 1500s, who asked how to split 8 measures of wine fairly using jugs of 5 and 3. Similar riddles were told even earlier, and the puzzle became famous again in the 1995 film Die Hard with a Vengeance.
