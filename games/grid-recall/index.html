Press Start, then remember the squares that light up.

How to play

Goal

Remember which squares light up, then tap them all.

How it works

  • Press Start. Some squares glow for a moment, then go dark.
  • Tap every square that glowed, in any order. Correct taps turn peach.
  • Find them all to move up a level: one more square lights up each level, and the grid grows as you go.
  • A wrong tap ends the round and shows the squares you missed. You have three tries per game.

Tips

  • Don't try to memorise squares one by one. See the whole pattern as a shape, like a letter or a constellation.
  • Split bigger patterns into two or three small clusters.
  • Take a breath before each round. There's no timer while you tap.

A little history

Tasks like this are used by psychologists to measure visual and spatial working memory: how much a person can hold in mind for a few seconds. Most adults can hold around five to nine items, and practice helps you use clever patterns to remember more.
