How to play

Goal

Slide the cars and trucks out of the way so the coral car can drive out of the gap on the right.

How it works

  • Cars only move forwards and backwards in the direction they face: sideways cars slide left and right, upright cars slide up and down. Nothing turns.
  • Swipe a car to slide it. Swipe further to slide it further. Or tap a car: if it can only go one way, it moves one square.
  • Each slide counts as one move. Try to match the fewest possible.
  • Levels 1 to 8 get harder, from 4 moves to 30.

Tips

  • Work backwards: which cars block the coral car's path, and what blocks those?
  • Trucks are long and need lots of space. Plan early where each one can go.
  • Sometimes you have to move the coral car backwards to make room.

The history of Traffic Jam

Sliding-car puzzles were popularised by Japanese puzzle master Nob Yoshigahara in the 1970s, and became a world-famous boxed puzzle in the 1990s. They are a favourite way to teach planning ahead.
