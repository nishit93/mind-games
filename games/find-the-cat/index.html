How to play

Goal

Find the cat in each picture. There are eight pictures, and each one is a little more crowded.

How it works

  • Look through the pictures and tap the cat 🐱 when you spot it.
  • If you tap another animal it wiggles, and you can keep looking. There is no timer and nothing to lose.
  • Find the cat in all eight pictures to finish the game.

Tips

  • Look along each row from left to right so you do not miss a corner.
  • The cat always has pointed ears and whiskers. Do not mix it up with the other animals!
  • Take your time. Looking carefully is the whole game.

The history of Find the Cat

Search-and-find pictures have delighted children for generations, from the crowded scenes of picture books to the "Where is…?" books of the late twentieth century. They train careful looking, attention and patience.
